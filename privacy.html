BeatTogether

Privacy Policy · Last updated: March 14, 2026

BeatTogether ("we", "the app") is built to share heart-rate data between people who care about each other. Protecting your privacy is our top priority.

1. Data We Collect

  • Apple ID identifier – used only to identify your account within the app. We never see your email or full Apple ID.
  • Heart-rate data – collected from Apple Watch via HealthKit and shared with your connected partner(s) in real time through Apple's CloudKit infrastructure.
  • Partner connections – the CloudKit record IDs of people you choose to connect with.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Heart-rate readings are stored in your private iCloud zone and shared only with partners you explicitly invite.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

3. Data Storage

All data is stored in Apple CloudKit (iCloud). Apple's servers are subject to Apple's own privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Heart-rate history older than 30 days is not retained in CloudKit by the app.

4. HealthKit

BeatTogether reads heart-rate data via HealthKit. This data is used exclusively to display your heart rate to your partner and is never shared beyond that.

5. Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time using Settings → Delete Account inside the app. This removes all CloudKit records associated with your account.

6. Children

BeatTogether is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.
